The percentage of households in the lowest income bracket and how much that percentage changed from 2012 to 2022 are the two most important factors when deciding which neighnorhood would best based on cost of living. The lower the percentage of households in the lowest income bracket and the higher the decrease in that percentage over 10 years, the more affordable a neighborhood is. Point Breeze North has only 7.8% in the lowest income bracket and went down 78.8% since 2012, making it the best neighborhood according to this data.
